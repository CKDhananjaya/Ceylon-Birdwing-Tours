04 Days Tour Packages

Tour Package - 01 (Culture & Heritage)

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport
  • Pick-up from Airport, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Visit Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress.
  • Enjoy an exciting Minneriya Jeep Safari.
  • Overnight stay in Sigiriya.
Day 2 : Sigiriya
  • Experience a traditional village tour.
  • Explore the Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Watch the sunset from Pidurangala Rock.
  • Overnight stay in Sigiriya.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Visit a Spice & Herbal Garden.
  • Explore a Silk Garden.
  • See the Temple of the Tooth Relic and Kandy Lake.
  • Walk through the Royal Botanical Gardens.
  • Visit the Ceylon Tea Museum.
  • Stop at the Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue and Kandy View Point.
  • Evening Kandyan Dance Show.
  • Overnight stay in Kandy.
Day 4 : Departure
  • Visit Gem & Mineral shops.
  • Experience the Millennium Elephant Foundation.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport, Port, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.

Tour Package - 02 (Coast & Sun)

Day 1 : Bentota
  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport
  • Pick-up from Airport, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
  • Travel to Bentota
  • Leisure at Bentota Beach (diving, snorkeling, water skiing, speed boating, kitesurfing, windsurfing).
  • Rejuvenate with a Traditional Ayurvedic Spa
  • Overnight stay in Bentota.
Day 2 : Galle
  • Travel to Galle
  • Take a boat safari along the Madu Ganga River.
  • Visit the Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery.
  • Explore Hikkaduwa Coral Gardens.
  • Stop by Gem & Mineral shops.
  • Discover the historic Galle Fort & Lighthouse.
  • Overnight stay in Galle.
Day 3 : Mirissa
  • Travel to Mirissa
  • Whale watching at Mirissa.
  • Parrot Rock & Coconut Tree Hill.
  • Enjoy beach activities in Mirissa.
  • Overnight stay in Mirissa.
Day 4 : Departure
  • Travel to Colombo and airport
  • Travel to Colombo for a city tour: Gangaramaya Temple, Independence Square, Lotus Tower, Red Masjid, Pettah Market, and shopping.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.

Tour Package - 03 (Highlands & Nature)

Day 1 : Nuwara Eliya
  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport
  • Pick-up from Airport, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
  • Travel to Nuwara Eliya
  • Visit Ramboda Waterfall.
  • Tea plantation & tea factory.
  • Stroll through Victoria Park.
  • Relax at Gregory Lake.
  • Visit Moon Plains and Seetha Amman Kovil.
  • See the Lover’s Leap Waterfall.
  • Overnight stay in Nuwara Eliya.
Day 2 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy
  • Tour the Ceylon Tea Museum and Gem Museum.
  • See the Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue and Kandy View Point.
  • Visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic and Kandy Lake.
  • Evening Kandyan Cultural Dancing Show.
  • Overnight stay in Kandy.
Day 3 : Sigiriya
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Stop at a Herbal & Spice Garden and silk garden.
  • Explore the Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Enjoy a traditional village tour.
  • Watch the sunset from Pidurangala Rock.
  • Experience Ayurveda massage and steam bath (recommended).
Day 4 : Final Safari
  • Climb the iconic Sigiriya Lion Rock.
  • Experience a Jeep Safari (Minneriya, Kaudulla, or Hurulu).
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport, Port, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
