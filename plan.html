Exclusive Plan - 01 4 Days

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport
  • Pick-up from Airport, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Experience a traditional village tour or cooking demonstration of Sri Lankan curries. We will enjoy village lunch we prepare.
  • Watch the sunset from Pidurangala Rock.
  • Overnight stay in Sigiriya.
Day 2 : Sigiriya
  • Early Morning visit Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • Safari at national park near by. Kaudulla, Minneriya or Hurulla national park.
  • Explore the Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • We recommended to experience Ayurveda massage and stem bath.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy
  • Visit a Spice & Herbal Garden.
  • Explore a Silk Garden.
  • Visit the Ceylon Tea Museum.
  • See the Temple of the Tooth Relic and Kandy Lake.
  • Evening Kandyan Dance Show.
  • Overnight stay in Kandy.
Day 4 : Kandy & Departure
  • Visit Gem & Mineral shops.
  • Stop at the Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue and Kandy View Point.
  • Walk through the Royal Botanical Gardens.
  • Experience the Millennium Elephant Foundation.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport,or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.

Exclusive Plan - 02 8 Days

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Pick-up from Colombo Airport, Port, or hotel.
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage.
  • Explore Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Sunset at Pidurangala Rock.
Day 2 : Sigiriya
  • Climb Sigiriya Lion Rock.
  • Experience a village tour.
  • Jeep Safari(Minneriya/Kawudulla/Hurulu).
  • Overnight in Sigiriya.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Travel to kandy
  • Visit a Spice Garden & Silk Garden
  • Ceylon Tea Museum & Gem Museum.
  • Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue & Kandy View Point.
  • Temple of the Tooth Relic & Kandy Lake.
  • Kandyan Dance Show.
  • Overnight in Kandy.
Day 4 : Nuwara Eliya
  • Ramboda Waterfalls & Hanuman Temple.
  • Tea plantation and factory.
  • City Tour of Nuwara Eliya (Victoria Park, Gregory Lake, Moon Plains, Strawberry Farm & Vegetable Market)
  • Lover’s Leap Waterfall.
  • Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.
Day 5 : Ella
  • Train ride from Nanuoya to Ella.
  • City Tour of Ella (Little Adam’s Peak, Ella Rock, Ella Gap, Nine Arch Bridge, Demodara Loop, Flying Ravana Zipline, Ravana Caves, Ravana Falls)
  • Overnight in Ella.
Day 6 : Yala & Weligama
  • Travel to Yala
  • Yala Jeep Safari.
  • Travel to Weligama
  • Leisure at Weligama Beach.
  • Overnight in Weligama.
Day 7 : Bentota
  • Travel to Galle
  • Visit Galle Dutch Fort.
  • Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery.
  • Madu Ganga Boat Safari.
  • Leasure at Bentota (diving, snorkeling, water skiing, speed boating, kitesurfing, windsurfing, banana boating,3)
  • Overnight in Bentota.
Day 8 : Departure
  • Travel to Colombo
  • Colombo City Tour (Gangaramaya Temple, Independence Square, Lotus Tower, Red Masjid, Pettah Market, shopping.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport, or hotel)
