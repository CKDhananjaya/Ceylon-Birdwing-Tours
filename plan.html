Exclusive Plan - 01 4 Days

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Pick-up from Airport, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
  • Travel to Sigiriya.
  • Traditional village tour or cooking demonstration.
  • Enjoy an authentic village lunch.
  • Sunset at Pidurangala Rock.
  • Overnight stay in Sigiriya.
Day 2 : Sigiriya & Wildlife
  • Early Morning visit Sigiriya Rock Fortress.
  • Safari at Minneriya, Kaudulla, or Hurulu National Park.
  • Explore the Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Recommended: Ayurveda massage and steam bath.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy.
  • Visit Spice & Herbal Garden and Silk Garden.
  • Visit the Ceylon Tea Museum.
  • Temple of the Tooth Relic & Kandy Lake.
  • Evening Kandyan Dance Show.
  • Overnight stay in Kandy.
Day 4 : Kandy & Departure
  • Visit Gem & Mineral shops.
  • Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue & View Point.
  • Walk through the Royal Botanical Gardens.
  • Millennium Elephant Foundation.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport or Hotel.

Exclusive Plan - 02 8 Days

Day 1 : Sigiriya Arrival
  • Pick-up from Colombo Airport, Port, or hotel.
  • Visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage.
  • Explore Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Sunset at Pidurangala Rock.
Day 2 : Sigiriya Adventure
  • Climb Sigiriya Lion Rock.
  • Experience a traditional village tour.
  • Jeep Safari (Minneriya/Kawudulla/Hurulu).
  • Overnight in Sigiriya.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy.
  • Visit Spice Garden & Silk Garden.
  • Ceylon Tea Museum & Gem Museum.
  • Temple of the Tooth Relic & Cultural Show.
Day 4 : Nuwara Eliya
  • Ramboda Waterfalls & Hanuman Temple.
  • Tea Plantation & Factory visit.
  • City Tour: Gregory Lake, Victoria Park, Strawberry Farm.
  • Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.
Day 5 : Ella
  • Scenic Train ride from Nanuoya to Ella.
  • Little Adam’s Peak & Nine Arch Bridge.
  • Flying Ravana Zipline & Ravana Falls.
  • Overnight in Ella.
Day 6 : Yala & Weligama
  • Yala National Park Jeep Safari.
  • Travel to Weligama.
  • Leisure at Weligama Beach.
  • Overnight in Weligama.
Day 7 : Bentota
  • Visit Galle Dutch Fort.
  • Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery & Madu Ganga Boat Safari.
  • Water sports at Bentota Beach.
  • Overnight in Bentota.
Day 8 : Departure
  • Colombo City Tour (Lotus Tower, Red Masjid, Pettah).
  • Shopping.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport or Hotel.
