The Ultimate Luxury Escape

Exclusive Plan - 01

08 Days / 07 Nights Complete Journey

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Pick-up from Colombo Airport, Port, or hotel.
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage.
  • Explore Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Sunset at Pidurangala Rock.
Day 2 : Sigiriya
  • Climb Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress.
  • Experience a traditional village tour.
  • Jeep Safari (Minneriya / Kaudulla / Hurulu).
  • Overnight in Sigiriya.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy
  • Visit a Spice Garden & Silk Garden.
  • Explore Ceylon Tea Museum & Gem Museum.
  • Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue & Kandy View Point.
  • Temple of the Tooth Relic & Kandy Lake.
  • Evening Kandyan Dance Show.
  • Overnight in Kandy.
Day 4 : Nuwara Eliya
  • Visit Ramboda Waterfalls & Hanuman Temple.
  • Tour a Tea plantation and factory.
  • City Tour of Nuwara Eliya: Victoria Park, Gregory Lake, Moon Plains.
  • Strawberry Farm & Vegetable Market.
  • Visit Lover’s Leap Waterfall.
  • Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.
Day 5 : Ella
  • Scenic Train ride from Nanuoya to Ella.
  • City Tour of Ella: Little Adam’s Peak, Ella Rock, Ella Gap.
  • Visit Nine Arch Bridge & Demodara Loop.
  • Flying Ravana Zipline, Ravana Caves & Ravana Falls.
  • Overnight in Ella.
Day 6 : Yala & Weligama
  • Travel to Yala
  • Yala National Park Jeep Safari.
  • Travel to Weligama.
  • Leisure at Weligama Beach.
  • Overnight in Weligama.
Day 7 : Bentota
  • Travel to Galle and visit Galle Dutch Fort.
  • Visit Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery.
  • Madu Ganga Boat Safari.
  • Leisure at Bentota: Diving, snorkeling, water skiing, speed boating, kitesurfing, windsurfing, banana boating.
  • Overnight in Bentota.
Day 8 : Departure
  • Travel to Colombo.
  • Colombo City Tour: Gangaramaya Temple, Independence Square, Lotus Tower.
  • Explore Red Masjid, Pettah Market & Shopping.
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport or hotel.
