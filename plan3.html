Tour Package 01: Scenic South & Highlands

  • Airport / Hotel Pick-up & Travel to Galle
  • Madu Ganga River Boat Safari
  • Visit Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery
  • Explore Galle Dutch Fort & Lighthouse
  • Overnight in Galle
Day 2 : Yala
  • Morning Whale Watching at Mirissa
  • Travel to Yala
  • Overnight stay in Yala
Day 3 : Ella
  • Morning Safari at Yala National Park
  • Travel to Ella
  • Hike Little Adam’s Peak & Ella Rock
  • Experience Flying Ravana Zipline
  • Visit Ravana Caves & Ravana Falls
  • Overnight stay in Ella
Day 4 : Nuwara Eliya
  • Visit Nine Arch Bridge & Demodara Loop
  • Scenic Train ride from Ella to Nanu oya
  • Pick up from Nanu oya & Travel to Nuwara Eliya
  • City tour of Nuwara Eliya
  • Overnight stay at Nuwara Eliya
Day 5 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy
  • Visit Tea Plantation, Factory & Ramboda Waterfalls
  • Explore Hanuman Temple & Kandy Tooth Temple
  • Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue & View Point
  • Kandy Dancing Show
  • Overnight stay in Kandy
Day 6 : Departure
  • Visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage
  • Explore a Spice Garden
  • Drop-off at Colombo Airport or Hotel
