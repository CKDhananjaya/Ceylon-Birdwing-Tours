05 Days Tour Package

Tour Package - 02 (Grand Heritage & Culture)

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport
  • Pick-up from Airport, or hotel in Colombo/Negombo.
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Experience a traditional village tour or cooking demonstration of Sri Lankan curries.
  • Enjoy the village lunch we prepare.
  • Watch the sunset from Pidurangala Rock.
  • Overnight stay in Sigiriya.
Day 2 : Sigiriya
  • Early Morning visit Sigiriya Rock Fortress (Lion Rock).
  • Safari at national park nearby: Kaudulla, Minneriya, or Hurulu national park.
  • Explore the Dambulla Cave Temple.
  • Recommended experience: Ayurveda massage and steam bath.
Day 3 : Kandy
  • Travel to Kandy
  • Visit a Spice & Herbal Garden.
  • Explore a Silk Garden.
  • Visit the Ceylon Tea Museum.
  • See the Temple of the Tooth Relic and Kandy Lake.
  • Evening Kandyan Dance Show.
  • Overnight stay in Kandy.
Day 4 : Kandy
  • Visit Gem & Mineral shops.
  • Stop at the Bahiravokanda Buddha Statue and Kandy View Point.
  • Walk through the Royal Botanical Gardens.
  • Overnight stay in Kandy.
Day 5 : Colombo & Departure
  • Travel to Colombo.
  • Visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage or Millennium Elephant Foundation (Elephant Walk Experience).
  • Colombo city tour: Gangaramaya Temple, Independence Square, Colombo Lotus Tower, Red Masjid, Pettah Market.
  • Shopping in Colombo.
  • Drop at Colombo Airport / Hotel in Colombo or Negombo.
