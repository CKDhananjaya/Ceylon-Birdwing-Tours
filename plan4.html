The Ultimate Island Experience

07 Days Tour Package

Tour Package 01: Signature Grand Tour

Day 1 : Sigiriya
  • Pick-up from Colombo Airport / Hotel in Colombo or Negombo
  • Travel to Sigiriya
  • Village tour with traditional lunch
  • Evening Jeep Safari at National park (Minneriya, Kaudulla, or Hurulu)
  • Overnight at Sigiriya
Day 2 : Kandy
  • Early morning climb the Sigiriya Lion Rock
  • Visit Dambulla Temple
  • Travel to Kandy
  • Spice Garden & Silk Garden visit
  • Gem Museum & Bahiravokanda Vihara Buddha Statue
  • Kandy Tooth Temple & Kandy Lake
  • Kandyan Dance Show & Overnight at Kandy
Day 3 : Nuwara Eliya
  • Travel to Nuwara Eliya
  • Visit Ramboda Waterfalls & Hanuman Temple
  • Tea factory & Tea plantation visit
  • Explore Victoria Park & Gregory Lake
  • Moon Plains & Strawberry Farm visit
  • Vegetable Market exploration
  • Overnight at Nuwara Eliya
Day 4 : Ella
  • Scenic Train Ride from Nuwara Eliya to Ella
  • Hike Little Adams Peak & Ella Rock
  • Visit Nine Arch Bridge & Demodara Loop
  • Flying Ravana Mega Zipline experience
  • Ravana Caves & Ravana Waterfalls
  • Overnight at Ella
Day 5 : Mirissa
  • Morning Jeep Safari at Yala National Park
  • Travel to Mirissa
  • Visit Parrot Rock & Coconut Tree Hill
  • Leisure at Mirissa Beach & Water Sports (surfing, wakeboarding, jet skiing, tubing)
  • Overnight at Mirissa
Day 6 : Bentota
  • Early morning Whale Watching at Mirissa
  • Travel to Bentota
  • Explore Galle Dutch Fort & Lighthouse
  • Visit Turtle Hatchery
  • Overnight at Bentota
Day 7 : Departure
  • Madu River Boat Safari
  • Travel to Colombo for City Tour
  • Ganagaramaya temple & Lotus tower
  • Red Masjid & Shopping at Pettah Market
  • Drop at Colombo Airport / Hotel
